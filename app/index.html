How a deal gets on this page

  1. Found today. Every deal was found on Black Friday, 27 November 2026, by reading the price at the store or in a deals roundup that names the store.
  2. Checked, not copied. The sale price, the usual price and the discount must add up. If they do not, the deal does not go up.
  3. Given a signal. NEW LOW means the lowest price recorded for that product. STRONG PRICE means a genuine cut worth buying. ROUTINE means a fair price, not a blowout. INFLATED means the “usual” price was raised to flatter the cut — none of those made the list.
  4. Linked to the store. Every “View at store” button opens the product at the store that sells it. Prices move during the sale — always confirm at checkout.
